    Ask the assistant

    Ask anything about riding transit here: how to pay, what a fare costs, how to use an elevator-friendly route. Answers come only from the agencies' published data, and say where they come from.

    This assistant is an independent, unofficial project. It plans trips from each agency's published timetables (found through Transitland), shows live departures where agencies publish them, and answers questions from the agencies' own fare and rider pages. Agency names are used only to identify their services. Transit changes; always confirm with the agency before you travel. What we keep: your questions and answers, map searches rounded to about 100 meters, the kind of device, and an anonymous code that tells one visitor's chats apart without identifying you, for up to one year. We don't keep names or IP addresses; email addresses and phone numbers are removed automatically.
