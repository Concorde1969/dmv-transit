    Ask the assistant

    Ask anything about riding transit here: how to pay, what a fare costs, how to use an elevator-friendly route. Answers come only from the agencies' published data, and say where they come from.

    This assistant is an independent, unofficial project. It plans trips from each agency's published timetables (found through Transitland), shows live departures where agencies publish them, and answers questions from the agencies' own fare and rider pages. Agency names are used only to identify their services. Transit changes; always confirm with the agency before you travel. What we keep: your questions and answers; map searches with start and end rounded to about 100 meters, the nearest stops, the names of landmarks or stations you pick (never street addresses or what you type) and the trip options shown; which options and panels you open; the kind of device and the site that sent you here; and an anonymous code that tells one visitor's activity apart without identifying you, for up to one year. We don't keep names or IP addresses; email addresses and phone numbers are removed automatically. Rider reports you send are shown to other riders and the assistant for a few hours, and kept for up to one year with the same anonymous code.
