Hello — how can I help you get around?

Ask a question in plain words about riding the bus, train, or Metro anywhere in the Washington DC, Maryland, and Virginia area. New to public transit? That's exactly who this is for.

Please note: this is an unofficial tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by WMATA (Metro) or any other transit agency. For official information, refunds, or complaints, contact the agency directly — for Metro, visit wmata.com.
About this site

This assistant is an independent project. It answers from public information published by the region's transit agencies — schedules, maps, fare pages, and WMATA's public data feeds — which it collects and stores regularly. It does not make things up from memory: if the information isn't in its data, it will say so.

Transit agencies change routes, schedules, and fares from time to time, and this site's copy can lag behind. Always confirm departure times and prices with the agency before you travel. Agency names are used only to identify their services.

What we keep: your questions and the assistant's answers, with the date, the kind of device (phone, tablet, or computer), and an anonymous code that tells one visitor's chats apart without identifying who you are. We do not keep your name or your internet (IP) address, and email addresses and phone numbers are removed automatically. We use this only to find and fix wrong or unclear answers, and delete it after one year.
